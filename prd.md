# Stillwater Fishing Game Requirements

## Core game

- Single-player fishing game.
- Catch different types of fish and collect coins.
- Spend coins to upgrade your fishing rod, bait, and boat.

## Modes and outcomes

- **Freeplay:** Relax and fish without a timer or ending.
- **Competition:** Catch a target number of fish before time runs out.
- Win the competition to earn bonus coins.
- Lose the competition if time runs out before reaching the target.

## Controls and environment

- Let the player control the boat.
- Make the water active and animated.
- Include weather conditions.
- When a fish is on the line, require rapid Space presses to reel it in.

## Implementation

- Keep the complete game in `game.html`.
- Format HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with readable indentation and line breaks.
